Before you play — two trophies can be lost

Strangers & Freaks (complete 8 stranger mission strands) and Actions Speak Louder (make all unique choices in the story) are rated Likely missable: their content is optional and one-off, and the official list promises no replay for either. The habits that save both cost nothing — start every stranger strand the moment you meet its owner, finish strands before pushing chapters, and keep a manual save before chapters end and before choices land. The reasoning behind every verdict, trophy by trophy, is on the Missables tab.

Sources. Trophy names, descriptions and grades: the official PlayStation 5 list as published via Exophase; progress targets PlayStation tracks: PSNProfiles.
